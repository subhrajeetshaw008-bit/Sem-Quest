How points work

Study, DSA and lab blocks earn 20 XP (Python and labs 15). Sleep on time earns 5 and bonus tasks 10. Finishing every focus block in a day gives 50 bonus XP. Each day in a row adds 10% to that day's XP, up to 2x. Future days stay locked until their date. On weekdays, Genshin unlocks after that day's focus blocks are done.

Rules: 10-min break after every 50 min of study. Sleep by 11:30 PM. If a day slips, use the Sunday recap hour as catch-up. From 16 Nov the second weekly session of each subject becomes a timed past paper. Dates assume exams start around 10 Dec. Progress is saved in this browser.
